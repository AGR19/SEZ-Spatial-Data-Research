1. JRC GLOBAL SURFACE WATER — Occurrence Layer (v1.4, 2021)
================================================================================

Source: European Commission Joint Research Centre (JRC)
Product: Global Surface Water Explorer — Occurrence dataset
URL base: http://storage.googleapis.com/global-surface-water/downloads2021/occurrence/
License: Free to use with attribution (Pekel et al., 2016, Nature)
Download script: /Users/adisheshravishankar/Downloads/downloadWaterData_PythonV3_2021/downloadWaterData_PythonV3.py

WHAT IT MEASURES:
  Percentage of time (1984-2021) that each pixel was classified as water.
  Values 0-100 represent water occurrence frequency.
  Useful for identifying permanent rivers, lakes, dams, and seasonal water.

TILE GRID:
  Global tiles are 10° × 10° in latitude/longitude.
  Naming convention: occurrence_[LON]_[LAT]v1_4_2021.tif
  Example: occurrence_120E_10Nv1_4_2021.tif
  The tile name refers to the SOUTHWEST corner of the tile.
  So "120E_10N" covers 120°E-130°E × 10°N-20°N.

SPATIAL RESOLUTION: 30 meters per pixel (Landsat-derived)
CRS: EPSG:4326 (WGS 84)
PIXEL DEPTH: 8-bit unsigned integer (0-100, 255=no data)

TILES DOWNLOADED FOR PHILIPPINES:

================================================================================

Source: University of Maryland (Hansen et al., 2013, Science)
Product: Global Forest Change 2000-2024, version 1.12

5. VIIRS NIGHTTIME LIGHTS — DOWNLOADED (V2.0, 2018)
================================================================================

Source: Earth Observation Group (EOG), Colorado School of Mines
Product: VIIRS Nighttime Lights Annual Composite V2.0, Year 2018
Product page: https://eogdata.mines.edu/products/vnl/
Download directory: https://eogdata.mines.edu/nighttime_light/annual/v20/2018/
License: CC-BY 4.0
Citation: Elvidge, C.D., et al. (2021). Annual time series of global VIIRS
          nighttime lights derived from monthly averages: 2012 to 2019.
          Remote Sensing, 13(5), 922.

WHY VERSION V2.0 AND YEAR 2018:
  - V2.0 was chosen because V2.2 (latest) is only available for 2012-2023
    and all versions require the same authentication.
  - Year 2018 was chosen to match temporal proximity with other datasets:
    * PEZA zone list is as of November 2017
    * Ravago et al. firm survey was conducted June-September 2019
    * The PEZA registered companies list (Reference [7] in Ravago paper)
      is from February 2018
    * Hansen forest data includes lossyear through 2024 but baseline is 2000
    * WorldPop and GHSL are available for 2020 but also for earlier years
  - Using 2018 provides the closest temporal match to our zone data.

WHAT IT MEASURES:
  Average radiance from nighttime satellite observations (VIIRS DNB sensor
  aboard Suomi NPP satellite), measured in nanowatts per square centimeter
  per steradian (nW/cm²/sr). Annual composites aggregate cloud-free monthly
  observations into yearly summary statistics.

SPATIAL RESOLUTION: 15 arc-seconds (~500 m at equator)
CRS: EPSG:4326 (WGS 84)
COVERAGE: 180°W-180°E, 75°N-65°S (global)
PIXEL DEPTH: Float32

FILES AVAILABLE IN 2018 DIRECTORY (all global):

  | File | Compressed | Description |
  |------|-----------|-------------|
  | ...average.tif.gz | 9.3 GB | Annual average radiance (UNMASKED — includes noise, gas flares, fires, aurora) |
  | ...cf_cvg.tif.gz | 1.9 GB | Cloud-free coverage count |
  | ...cvg.tif.gz | 286 MB | Total observation coverage count |
  | ...maximum.tif.gz | 8.9 GB | Maximum radiance observed |
  | ...median.tif.gz | 9.2 GB | Median radiance (UNMASKED) |
  | ...minimum.tif.gz | 7.2 GB | Minimum radiance observed |
  | ...average_masked.tif.gz | 271 MB | Annual average radiance (MASKED — background noise removed) |
  | ...lit_mask.tif.gz | 21 MB | Binary mask of persistently lit areas |
  | ...median_masked.tif.gz | 270 MB | Median radiance (MASKED) |

WHY WE CHOSE THESE TWO FILES:

  (a) average_masked.tif.gz (271 MB compressed → 11 GB uncompressed)

      This is the PRIMARY analysis file. "Masked" means:
      - Background areas (no persistent lighting) are set to zero
      - Removes noise from gas flares, fires, volcanoes, aurora
      - Removes ephemeral light sources
      - Only retains pixels in "persistently lit" areas
      This is what the academic literature uses for measuring economic
      activity. The unmasked version (9.3 GB) includes all noise and
      would require additional processing to remove non-economic light.

      Pixel values: Radiance in nW/cm²/sr
        - 0 = no persistent lighting (masked background)
        - Positive values = average annual radiance
        - Higher values = more intense/consistent nighttime lighting
        - Typical urban range: 5-200 nW/cm²/sr
        - Industrial areas: often 50-300+ nW/cm²/sr

  (b) cvg.tif.gz (286 MB compressed → 5.4 GB uncompressed)

      This is the QUALITY CONTROL file. It counts the total number of
      valid observations per pixel during the year. Pixels with low
      coverage (few cloud-free observations) have less reliable radiance
      estimates. Use this to:
      - Filter out pixels with < N observations (e.g., < 10)
      - Weight analysis by observation count
      - Identify data quality issues in cloudy regions (Philippines
        is in the tropics — cloud cover is a real concern)

      Pixel values: Integer count of valid observations
        - Range: 0 to ~365 (theoretical max = nightly observations)
        - Typical: 20-80 in tropical regions (due to cloud cover)
        - < 10: Unreliable, consider excluding

FILES DOWNLOADED:

  | File                                                                    | Compressed | Uncompressed | Resolution  | CRS       |
  |-------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------|-------------|-------------|-----------|
  | VNL_v2_npp_2018_global_vcmslcfg_c202102150000.average_masked.tif       | 271 MB    | 11 GB       | 15 arcsec   | EPSG:4326 |
  | VNL_v2_npp_2018_global_vcmslcfg_c202101211500.cvg.tif                  | 286 MB    | 5.4 GB      | 15 arcsec   | EPSG:4326 |
  |                                                                         |           | 16.4 GB     |             |           |

  Local path: raw datasets/viirs/

FILE NAMING CONVENTION:
  VNL_v2_npp_2018_global_vcmslcfg_c202102150000.average_masked.tif
  │     │   │    │      │         │              │
  │     │   │    │      │         │              └─ statistic type
  │     │   │    │      │         └─ processing timestamp (YYYYMMDDHHMMSS)
  │     │   │    │      └─ configuration (VIIRS Cloud Mask Stray Light Corrected Filtered Gap-filled)

  This is because the masked products undergo additional processing.

DOWNLOAD METHOD:
  Manual browser download after OAuth2 authentication at eogdata.mines.edu.
  Files were downloaded as .tif.gz (gzip compressed), then uncompressed.

ISSUES ENCOUNTERED:
  - ALL VIIRS VNL products require OAuth2 authentication. HTTP requests
    without auth return 302 redirect to OpenID Connect login page at
    https://eogauth-new.mines.edu/realms/eog/protocol/openid-connect/auth
  - This applies to ALL versions (V2.0, V2.1, V2.2) — no unauthenticated
    access exists for any version.
  - No public mirror found. NOAA's old NGDC portal redirects to EOG.
  - Google Cloud Storage has no public VIIRS bucket.
  - This is the ONLY dataset in our collection that requires registration.
  - Registration is free and instant (email + password).
  - The uncompressed files are VERY LARGE (11 GB and 5.4 GB) because
    they are global at 15 arcsec resolution = ~86,400 × 16,800 pixels
    of Float32 data. In R, these should be loaded with terra::rast()
    and immediately cropped to Philippines extent to manage memory.

FILES WE DID NOT DOWNLOAD AND WHY:
  - average.tif.gz (9.3 GB): UNMASKED — includes background noise, gas
    flares, fires, biomass burning, aurora, and other non-economic light.
    Requires extensive manual masking. The masked version is pre-cleaned.
  - median.tif.gz (9.2 GB): UNMASKED median — same noise issues.
  - median_masked.tif.gz (270 MB): Valid alternative to average_masked.
    Median is more robust to outliers (single bright events). Could
    download later if average_masked shows outlier issues.
  - maximum.tif.gz (8.9 GB): Maximum radiance — dominated by outlier
    events, not useful for average economic activity measurement.
  - minimum.tif.gz (7.2 GB): Minimum radiance — mostly zeros/near-zero.
  - cf_cvg.tif.gz (1.9 GB): Cloud-free observation count (more specific
    than total coverage). Could download if quality control requires it.
  - lit_mask.tif.gz (21 MB): Binary mask of persistently lit areas.
    Small file — could download later if needed for masking.

USE CASE IN OUR ANALYSIS:
  Night lights are a well-established proxy for economic activity in
  development economics (Henderson, Storeygard & Weil, 2012). We use
  average_masked radiance to:
  1. Compare mean nighttime radiance in zone vs non-zone municipalities
  2. Potential Part 4 temporal extension using multi-year composites
  Night lights are explicitly listed in the assignment as an example
  variable for comparing zone and non-zone locations.
