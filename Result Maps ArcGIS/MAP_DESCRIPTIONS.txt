ArcGIS Pro Result Maps - Description
=====================================

Map 1: Zone Locations (Map 1 Zone Locations.pdf)
  Shows the geographic distribution of all 102 industrial zones across
  the Philippines, color-coded by zone type (manufacturing, agro-industrial,
  freeport). Demonstrates the heavy concentration in the CALABARZON corridor
  south of Metro Manila (Cavite, Laguna, Batangas) and secondary clusters
  in Cebu and Davao.

Map 2: Zone vs Non-Zone Choropleth (Map2 Zone vs Non Zone.pdf)
  Municipality boundaries colored by mean nighttime light radiance (VIIRS 2018).
  Zone locations overlaid as points. Visually confirms that industrial zones
  are located in brighter (more economically active) municipalities.

Map 3: Infrastructure and Distances (Map3 Infrastructure and Distances.pdf)
  Shows major road networks (trunk/primary/motorway from OpenStreetMap) and
  airport locations overlaid with zone points. Demonstrates that zones cluster
  along major transport corridors and near airports.

Map 4: Protected Areas and Zones (Map4 Protected Areas and Zones.pdf)
  Protected and conserved areas (WDPCA/IUCN) shown as green polygons with
  zone locations overlaid. Identifies potential environmental constraints
  where industrial zones are located near protected ecosystems.
